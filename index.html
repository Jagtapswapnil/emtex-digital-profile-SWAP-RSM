About EMTEX

One partner from part identification to connected data.

EMTEX Industrial Solutions brings together industrial marking, engraving, laser cutting and welding, special-purpose automation, vision, customized software and traceability. The objective is straightforward: give every component a permanent identity and connect that identity with production and quality data.

Solutions are selected around the actual component, material, mark requirement, cycle time, line condition and data architecture—then supported through trials, integration, training and after-sales service.

Application-led selectionComponent trials and process review before recommendation.
Customized integrationStandalone machines, SPMs, robotics, vision and line connectivity.
